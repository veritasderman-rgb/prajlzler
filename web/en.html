How it works

Four steps and you are back on the road.

  1. Call or write

    Tell us where you are (hotel, street, car park) and what happened. A photo of the warning light or the tyre helps.

  2. Advice on the phone

    Often you can drive to us yourself. If not, we arrange a tow truck.

  3. Diagnosis and price up front

    We look at the car, explain in plain words what is wrong and tell you the price. Then you decide.

  4. Repair as fast as possible

    We know you have a check-out or a train to catch. For bigger repairs you go back to the spa town and we call when the car is ready.

What we help guests with most often

From a flat tyre to towing, all under one roof.

  • Flat tyre

    Repair or tyre replacement including balancing, tyre sales.

  • Car won't start

    Battery, starter, alternator: we test, jump-start and replace.

  • Warning light

    We read the fault memory and tell you whether it is safe to drive on.

  • Air conditioning

    Leak test, refill and disinfection of the air conditioning.

  • Electrics

    Central locking, window lifters, lights, fuses, diagnostics.

  • Brakes and suspension

    If it squeals or rattles: we check and repair what is needed for a safe journey home.

  • Auto glass

    Repair and replacement of windows, handling with your insurer.

  • Towing

    If the car cannot be driven, we arrange transport to our workshop.

Frequently asked questions from spa guests

My car won't start in Marienbad. What should I do?

Call AutoPraizler M.L. on +420 728 920 290 and tell us where you are. We check the battery, starter and alternator; if the car cannot be driven, we arrange a tow to our workshop in Mariánské Lázně.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the fault. Before any repair we inspect the car, explain in plain words what is wrong and tell you the price in advance. Then you decide.

I have a flat tyre. Can you help at short notice?

Yes. We repair the tyre or replace it including balancing, and we also sell new tyres. Where possible, a quick repair is done while you wait.

Where is the workshop and when is it open?

AutoPraizler M.L., U Mlékárny 844/8A, 353 01 Mariánské Lázně (Marienbad), near the railway station. Open Monday to Friday 8:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00, at weekends by phone arrangement.

Can I go back to the spa town while you repair the car?

Yes. For bigger repairs you go back to the spa town and we call you as soon as the car is ready.
