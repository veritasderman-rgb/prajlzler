How to find us

By the station, parking in front of the workshop

  • By car: from Cheb or Plzeň take road 21 to Mariánské Lázně. In the Úšovice district, by the railway station, turn into U Mlékárny street. The entrance is signposted.
  • From the spa centre: trolleybus to the Nádraží (railway station) stop, then two minutes on foot.
  • Parking: right in front of the workshops, vans included.
  • Entrance height: 3.3 m.
