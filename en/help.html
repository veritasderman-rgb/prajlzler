How it works

Four steps and you are on your way

  1. Call or message us

    Tell us where you are (hotel, street, car park) and what happened. Send a photo if you can: the warning light, the tyre, a puddle under the car.

  2. Advice on the phone

    Often you can drive to us yourself. If not, we arrange a tow truck.

  3. Diagnosis and price up front

    We look at the car, explain plainly what is wrong and tell you the price. Then you decide.

  4. Repair as fast as possible

    We know you have a check-out or a train. Small jobs while you wait. For bigger repairs, go into town and we call you when it is done.

Frequently asked

What visitors ask us

How fast will it be?

Tyres, batteries or bulbs usually while you wait. Repairs that need a part usually by the next day. We give you a realistic time right after the inspection.

Do you help at weekends?

Officially we are closed at weekends, but call. When we can, we help, as our reviews show. We cannot promise it every time.

What if you don't have the part in stock?

Common parts for most makes arrive by the next day. Meanwhile we tell you whether the car is safe to use, or arrange a courtesy car.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, card or cash.

I have breakdown cover. Will my insurer pay?

Often yes. Call us first, we will tell you how to proceed and issue the invoice the way your insurer needs it.
